RedList Rescue Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026
RedList Rescue is developed and operated by Samantha Lynch.

The short version: RedList Rescue does not collect any personal information through the app. The app shows listings of shelter animals that are at risk of euthanasia ("red-listed"). It has no accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking. The listings are bundled with the app, and the ZIP code you type to search is used only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Information we collect

We collect no personal data through the RedList Rescue app. Specifically:

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address, or any other personal information inside the app.
  • The app contains no advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.
  • The app includes no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The animal listings shown in the app are bundled with the app itself. Browsing and searching them sends no information to us or to anyone else.

The ZIP code you search with

To show animals near you, the app asks for a ZIP code and a search radius. That ZIP code is used only on your device, to work out which of the bundled listings are nearby. It is never transmitted to us, never stored anywhere we can see, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, anything the app kept on the device is deleted with it.

Forms and links that leave the app

The app includes "join" and "submit a listing" forms and links to animal shelters. Those forms and pages live on the RedList Rescue website, not inside the app itself. If you choose to submit a form, the information you type into it (for example a shelter name and contact email) is sent through our form provider (Web3Forms) to the RedList Rescue inbox so we can follow up — that only happens when you fill in and submit a form yourself. Links to shelters and other outside pages open those organizations' own websites, which have their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for how those sites handle information.

Where the listings come from

The animal listings come from shelters' own published urgent lists and are bundled with the app when it is built. The app does not pull your information from any source, and no information about you is combined with the listings.

Sharing of information

We do not collect personal information through the app, so there is nothing for us to sell, rent, or share with third parties. Information you voluntarily send us through a website form is used only to respond to that submission (for example, to list a shelter's urgent animals).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If a future version of the app were to collect any information, this policy would be updated to describe it before that version is released.
