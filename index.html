For Shelters — get your red-listed animals seen

RedList Rescue is free for shelters, always. It exists for one thing: putting the animals with the least time left in front of the adopters, rescues, and fosters nearby. Create an account and add your animals yourself — every listing carries a live deadline countdown, so nobody has to guess who's most urgent.

  • 🚨 Only red-listed animals — your most urgent cases aren't buried under thousands of regular listings.
  • ⏰ Deadline countdowns on every animal you add.
  • 📍 Found by ZIP — nearby adopters, rescues, and fosters see your animals first.
  • ✅ Shelters are verified before their listings are featured — in this demo, new partners appear right away with a “Partner — pending verification” badge.

Support RedList Rescue

Two separate ways to help — pick either (or both). Every dollar is accounted for plainly, below.

❤️ Help keep RedList Rescue running

Donations toward the site's and app's running costs — hosting, the app store fees, and keeping the listings up to date — so the animals here stay visible.

🐱 Donate toward local TNR

Trap-neuter-return for feral and community cats in the SE Minnesota area — the humane way to stop the next generation of kittens from ending up on a list like this one. Recipient: a local TNR program — recipient to be named at launch.

Please read before donating: RedList Rescue is not a registered nonprofit, so donations are not tax-deductible. Donations to keep RedList Rescue running go to Sami / RedList Rescue and are used for the site's and app's running costs. TNR donations are passed through in full to the named local TNR effort above.
